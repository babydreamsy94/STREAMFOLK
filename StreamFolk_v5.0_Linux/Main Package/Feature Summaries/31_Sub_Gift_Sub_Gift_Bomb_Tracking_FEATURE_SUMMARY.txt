==============================================================================
SUB/GIFT SUB/GIFT BOMB TRACKING - FEATURE SUMMARY
==============================================================================

PURPOSE
Tracks subscriptions, resubscriptions, gift subscriptions, and gift bombs.

SYSTEM FLOW
Stores per-user counts and detailed labels including gifter names. Retains Prime/tier/month detail and gift-bomb child-event deduplication. Gift recipient entries in subscription reporting do not themselves imply attendance.

TRIGGERS / ENTRY POINT
Twitch Subscription, Twitch Resubscription, Twitch Gift Subscription, Twitch Gift Bomb

CONFIGURATION
Keep all four subscription triggers connected to this tracker. Attendance Tracker separately receives the corresponding support events and counts only eligible actors.
